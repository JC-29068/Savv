How It Works

Simple steps to make a difference with your surplus food

1

Register & Login

Restaurants and NGOs create accounts on our platform

2

List Available Food

Restaurants add details about surplus food including type, quantity, and pickup time

3

Receive Notifications

NGOs get instant alerts about available donations in their area

4

Collect & Distribute

NGOs collect the food and update the status, then distribute to those in need

Who Uses Savva?

Our platform serves three main user groups

Food Donors

  • Upload information about surplus food
  • Specify food type, quantity, and pickup time
  • Mark donations as Available or Collected
  • Track your impact on reducing food waste

NGOs / Volunteers

  • View available donations in your area
  • Select donations you can collect
  • Update status once food is collected
  • Coordinate distribution to those in need

Admin

  • Manage user registrations and verification
  • Monitor donation data and platform activity
  • Generate reports on food donation impact
  • Ensure platform security and integrity
